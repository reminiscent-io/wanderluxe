# Brag Plan: WanderLuxe (vertical cut)

A 9:16 cut of the landscape brag in `brag-output/`. Same story, copy, music, and beat locks. Every scene is re-laid out for a phone held upright and for social feeds, where platform UI covers the top and bottom of the frame.

## What is this app?
WanderLuxe is a free, collaborative trip planner: flights, hotels, dinners and days sit on one itinerary the whole group can see and edit, AI reads a pasted booking confirmation onto the right day, and the $3.99/mo Print Studio designs the finished plan as a printable keepsake.

## The angle
**From spreadsheet to keepsake,** told top to bottom. One real trip (Tokyo, Oct 3 to 5) goes from a cluttered spreadsheet to a printed edition. In the vertical frame the story also flows downward: the confirmation is read at the top, and the flight drops down into its day below. It is shot like a travel-magazine feature, with cream paper, bronze ink and serif headlines.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The group-chat line "wait, where are we staying again?" and a cramped Tokyo spreadsheet fill the upper half. The landing page's line lands in large serif across the lower half: **"Your journey deserves more than a spreadsheet."**

## Key moments (the middle)
- Two spreadsheet rows lift off the grid and drop down into the day card as its rows.
- The Trip Assistant (top) reads "ANA 175 confirmation.pdf". After one click on Import, the flight card falls into the day card (bottom) as the 11:05 AM row, pushing the evening down, on the beat where the full groove enters.
- Three real app screens fan out like dealt cards (Timeline left, Map right, Calendar landing on top in the center) under "Plan together, in real time."
- The free PDF, then three AI editions of the same trip swapping in as stacked prints: Edo Blue October, Tokyo Deco Evenings, Autumn Greens.

## Outro / punchline
The closing band (earth-500) wipes up. A stacked, centered lockup: "WanderLuxe", a rule, "Plan the trip together.", the sunset button "Start planning, free", and "wanderluxe.io".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** attach "ANA 175 confirmation.pdf" in the Trip Assistant.
2. **Key action:** it comes back as "Flight: ANA 175 · San Francisco (SFO) → Tokyo (HND) · Oct 3 · 11:05". Click **Import**.
3. **Result:** the flight lands at 11:05 AM on Saturday, October 3, the right day, on the shared itinerary.
